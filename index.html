About Us

Manufacturers First, Suppliers Second

Fort Trading was founded in 2020 with one goal: to make sourcing and fabrication simple, transparent, and reliable. Today our core business is the manufacture of aluminium and door products — designed, fabricated, and finished in our own workshop — supported by a hardware supply division that serves contractors, retailers, and industrial clients.

Aluminium & Door Manufacturing Primary

Our main division. We fabricate aluminium windows, doors, sliding systems, shopfronts, and custom profiles to specification — including contract-scale production for commercial and institutional projects.

Hardware Supply Secondary

We source, wholesale, and distribute hardware, tools, fasteners, safety gear, and industrial supplies from vetted manufacturers worldwide. We do not manufacture hardware — we supply it.

Based in Pietermaritzburg, South Africa, we work with developers, contractors, architects, retailers, and industrial buyers across Africa and beyond — building long-term partnerships based on trust, quality, and consistency.

Our Mission

To manufacture dependable aluminium and door solutions — and supply the hardware that keeps projects moving forward.
