About Us

One Company, Two Divisions

Fort Trading was founded in 2020 with one goal: to make sourcing simple, transparent, and reliable. Today we run two focused divisions that serve very different customers — but share the same commitment to quality, consistency, and honest pricing.

Hardware Supply

We source, wholesale, and distribute hardware, tools, fasteners, safety gear, and industrial supplies from vetted manufacturers worldwide. We do not manufacture hardware — we supply it, and we stand behind every item we ship.

Bed Linen Manufacturing

We design, cut, sew, and finish our own range of bed linen in-house — from standard retail sets to custom sizing for hotels, guesthouses, hospitals, and clinics.

Based in Pietermaritzburg, South Africa, we work with manufacturers, distributors, retailers, and hospitality groups across Africa and beyond — building long-term partnerships based on trust, quality, and consistency.

Our Mission

To supply reliable hardware and produce dependable bed linen that keep businesses and projects moving forward.
